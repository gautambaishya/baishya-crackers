About the shop

From Dalibari to your doorstep.

Baishya Crackers has operated out of the same corner shop near Dalibari High School since 1994, sourcing directly from licensed manufacturing units around Sivakasi — the town that makes roughly nine out of every ten fireworks sold in the country.

Every batch that reaches our shelves carries PESO explosive licence markings and is tested for noise and emission limits before it ships. We stock loose items for small family orders and pre-packed combo boxes for larger celebrations, housing societies and event organisers.

Before you light up

Safety guidelines we ask every customer to follow.

Standard precautions drawn from PESO explosive-safety advisories, printed on the inside of every box we sell.

Burst in open ground only. Never inside a room, balcony, corridor or near dry vegetation.

Keep water or sand within reach. A filled bucket next to every lighting point, not just one for the group.

Light one at a time, at arm's length. Use an incense stick or taper, not a matchstick held by hand.

Never relight a dud. Douse it in water and set it aside for at least 30 minutes before disposal.

Children only under adult supervision. Even sparklers burn at over 1,000°C at the tip.

Check local timing rules. Most states restrict bursting to fixed evening hours during festival season.

Store unused stock in a cool, dry place away from open flame — and away from where children can reach it unsupervised.
